About

Light, form, quiet confidence.

Elena Belousova is a European fashion photographer working between Barcelona and Paris. Her images move between editorial storytelling and commercial campaigns — built on soft light, sculptural composition and a calm, self-assured femininity.

Selected work has been published across leading fashion titles and shown during the international fashion weeks.
